Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

PocketPuzzle is a free browser puzzle game. This policy explains what little data the game touches — the short version: your scores stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your best scores, game mode, and sound preference in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (for example, by copying your daily result). Clearing your browser data removes it.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, anonymous usage statistics (such as page views and general interaction counts) may be collected via Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization. No advertising identifiers or personal profiles are created by this site itself.

Advertising

This game is funded by advertising. When ads are shown, third-party ad partners (such as Google AdSense or the game portal hosting the game) may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. You can control personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or your browser settings.

Children

The game contains no mature content. Where required by law, ads served to users believed to be children follow applicable policies such as those of the hosting platform.

Your choices

  • Clear local storage in your browser to erase saved scores and preferences.
  • Opt out of personalized Google ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • Contact us with any privacy question about this game.
